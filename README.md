# Economic Policy Lab

An interactive supply-and-demand graph for ECON 20802 / ECON 20802A (HEC Montréal). Set the two curves, then apply price ceilings and floors, quotas, taxes, subsidies and externalities, and read the traded outcome, consumer and producer surplus, government revenue and deadweight loss.

Un graphique interactif d'offre et de demande pour ECON 20802 / ECON 20802A (HEC Montréal) : prix plafond et plancher, quotas, taxes, subventions et externalités, avec les surplus et la perte sèche. L'interface est en anglais.

<https://econhec.github.io/policy-lab/>

## Model

- Demand is P = a − bQ and supply is P = c + dQ, each entered as two points. Neither curve goes below P = 0.
- Pc is the price buyers pay and Pp the price sellers receive, taxes and subsidies included.
- Price ceilings and floors apply to the market price, before taxes and subsidies.
- Under a binding quota, sellers receive the demand price, so the quota rent is part of producer surplus.
- Under a ceiling or floor, the units traded go to the highest-value buyers and the lowest-cost sellers.
- SMB = demand + consumption externality; SMC = supply − production externality. Q* is where SMB = SMC, and deadweight loss is the total surplus lost relative to Q*.
