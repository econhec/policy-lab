Pc is the price buyers pay and Pp the price sellers receive, taxes and subsidies included. Price ceilings and floors apply to the market price, before taxes and subsidies. Under a binding quota, sellers receive the demand price, so the quota rent is part of producer surplus.
Total surplus = CS + PS + Government (net) + External benefit. DWL is the total surplus lost relative to the optimal Q*. If no externalities, Q* is the free private-market equilibrium. With externalities, Q* is where SMB = SMC.
